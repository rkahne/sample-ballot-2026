About the November 3, 2026 General Election

NEW FOR 2026

Louisville Metro Mayor and Metro Council races are nonpartisan elections — party labels will not appear on your official ballot for these races. The Louisville Democratic Party endorses candidates in these nonpartisan races and supports all registered Democrats in partisan races.

For partisan races (federal, state, and county offices), party labels WILL appear on your official ballot. For nonpartisan races (Mayor and Metro Council), candidate party registration is shown here for your information but will NOT appear on the official ballot.

🔒 Your candidate selections are not recorded or stored by the Louisville Democratic Party. This tool runs entirely in your browser.
